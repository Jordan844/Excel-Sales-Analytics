# 📊 Sales & Finance Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Sales and Finance data using Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, and DAX to generate actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 📈 Sales Report

### 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Create a **Customer Performance Report**.
2. Conduct a comprehensive comparison between **Market Performance** and **Sales Targets**.

### 🚀 Purpose of Sales Analytics

Empower businesses to monitor, evaluate, and optimize their sales performance effectively.

### 🔍 Importance of Sales Data Analysis

* Identify sales trends and patterns.
* Track key performance indicators (KPIs).
* Measure customer and market performance.

### 💡 Business Value

The generated reports help organizations:

* Determine effective customer discount strategies.
* Support negotiations with customers.
* Identify high-potential markets for business expansion.

---

# 💰 Finance Report

### 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Create **Profit & Loss (P&L) Reports** by:

   * Fiscal Year
   * Months
2. Create **Profit & Loss (P&L) Reports** by Markets.

### 🚀 Purpose of Financial Analytics

Support strategic decision-making through the evaluation of financial performance and effective stakeholder communication.

### 🔍 Importance of Financial Data Analysis

* Benchmark performance against previous periods and industry standards.
* Support budgeting and forecasting processes.
* Identify growth opportunities and financial risks.

### 💡 Business Value

The reports help organizations:

* Align financial planning with strategic objectives.
* Improve financial visibility and transparency.
* Strengthen confidence in the company's financial outlook.
